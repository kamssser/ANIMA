# Content Taxonomy — EDU-MOTION STUDIO

Use these categories to classify ideas. Each video should have one primary category and may have one secondary tag.

1. **Science & Nature** — biology, physics, chemistry, animals, ecosystems, weather, natural events.
2. **Hidden History** — overlooked events, historical inventions, turning points, archaeology, lost civilizations.
3. **What If Scenarios** — hypothetical situations, alternate conditions, extreme thought experiments.
4. **Space & Universe** — astronomy, planets, stars, galaxies, cosmology, spaceflight.
5. **Geography & World** — countries, borders, maps, oceans, climate, resources, population.
6. **Human Psychology** — memory, perception, cognition, habits, group behavior, decision-making.
7. **Technology & Inventions** — engineering, computing, internet, AI, machines, infrastructure, inventions.
8. **Economics & Society** — money, trade, banking, incentives, institutions, social systems.
9. **Mystery & Unsolved** — documented unresolved cases and unexplained observations; distinguish evidence from speculation.
10. **Everyday Science** — ordinary objects, foods, sounds, transport, household systems, and daily experiences.

## Topic selection score (1–5 each)
- Curiosity: Does the question create a strong reason to keep watching?
- Clarity: Can it be explained in one central idea?
- Visual potential: Can the mechanism be shown clearly?
- Evidence: Are reliable sources available?
- Relevance: Will the intended audience understand why it matters?

Prefer topics with high curiosity, visual potential, and evidence. Reject ideas that rely on misinformation or a misleading premise.
